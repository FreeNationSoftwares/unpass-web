2×2 Photo Size in Pixels: 600×600 for US Passports, Visas & IDs

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Photo & Signature Resizer tool →

'2×2 photo' is the standard size for US passports and visas, and many other ID cards and applications ask for it too. Here is what it means in pixels and how to make one from any photo.

Step by step

  1. Take a clear photo — Plain white or off-white background, face the camera, even light.
  2. Open the resizer — Select your photo.
  3. Pick 'ID Photo 2×2 in' — It sets 600×600 pixels.
  4. Resize and download — The photo is centre-cropped to a square.
  5. Print or upload — Print at 300 DPI for exactly 2×2 inches, or upload the file.
💡 For a printed 2×2 photo, set your printer to 'actual size' (100%). 'Fit to page' will make it the wrong size.

2×2 inches in pixels

  • 300 DPI (print): 600×600 px
  • US digital passport/visa: 600×600 to 1200×1200 px, JPEG, up to 240 KB
  • Head size: 1 to 1⅜ inches (25–35 mm) from chin to top of head

Where 2×2 photos are used

US passports and visas, US Green Card and naturalization forms, and many other countries' visa applications. Always check the specific rules on the official website of the office you are applying to.

FAQ

How many pixels is a 2×2 photo?

600×600 pixels at 300 DPI.

Is 2×2 the same as 51×51 mm?

Yes, 2 inches is about 51 mm.

Can I make a 2×2 photo at home?

Yes. Take a photo against a plain light wall, resize it to 600×600 and print at 300 DPI.
