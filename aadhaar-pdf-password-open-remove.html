What is the Aadhaar PDF password?

The e-Aadhaar password is the first 4 letters of your name (in CAPITAL letters, exactly as printed on Aadhaar) followed by your year of birth (YYYY).

💡 Example: name RAVI KUMAR, born 1998 → password is RAVI1998. If your name is shorter than 4 letters, use the full name plus the birth year.
