About Unpass

Unpass is a free set of online tools for PDFs and photos, made by FreeNation Softwares. We built it because most "free" PDF websites upload your private documents to their servers. Unpass does not. Every tool runs inside your own browser, so your bank statements, ID documents and exam photos never leave your device.

Our tools

Unlock, merge, split and compress PDFs, convert between images and PDF, and resize photos and signatures to the exact size that exam and government forms need.

Also on Android

Unpass is available as an Android app too, with the same private, offline tools.
