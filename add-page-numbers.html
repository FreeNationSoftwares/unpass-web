About Page Numbers

Add page numbers to a PDF in one click. Choose the style and position, and download a numbered copy — handy for reports, assignments and notes.

Questions

Where do the numbers go?

Wherever you choose — bottom or top, left, center or right.

Can I start from a different number?

The count starts at 1 by default and numbers every page in order.
