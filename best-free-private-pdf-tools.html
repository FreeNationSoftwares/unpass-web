Best Free PDF Tools That Don't Upload Your Files

When you use a typical free PDF website, your file is uploaded to someone else's server, processed there, and downloaded back. For a meme that's fine — for a bank statement, Aadhaar, salary slip or contract, it's a real privacy risk.

Why browser-based tools are safer

A browser-based tool does all the work on your own device using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your computer or phone, and nothing is stored. When you close the tab, it's gone.

What to look for

  • Says clearly that processing happens in your browser
  • No sign-up required
  • Works offline after the page loads
  • No file-size limits imposed by a server
