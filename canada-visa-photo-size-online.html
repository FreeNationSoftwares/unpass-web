Canada Visa Photo Size for Online Applications (IRCC)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Photo & Signature Resizer tool →

Visitor visa, study permit and work permit applications made online through IRCC ask you to upload a digital photo. Getting the size and background right avoids a request to resubmit.

Step by step

  1. Take the photo — Plain white or light background, even lighting, neutral expression.
  2. Open the resizer — Select your photo.
  3. Pick 'Canada Visa' — It sets 420×540 px automatically.
  4. Resize — The photo is centre-cropped to the right shape.
  5. Upload to your IRCC application — Use the JPEG file you downloaded.
💡 Keep the camera at eye level and leave a little space above your head so the crop does not cut your hair.

Digital photo requirements (online visa)

  • Size: minimum 420×540 pixels
  • Format: JPEG / JPG
  • Background: plain white or light coloured
  • Face: centred, looking straight at the camera, neutral expression
  • Recent: taken within the last 6 months

Passport and PR card photos follow different, stricter rules (printed 50×70 mm photos from a photographer). Always confirm on the official IRCC website.

FAQ

What size is a Canada visa photo in pixels?

At least 420×540 pixels for online applications.

Can I use a phone photo?

Yes, if it meets the background, lighting and size rules.

Can I wear glasses?

It is safest to remove them to avoid glare.
