CUET Photo & Signature Size (2026): KB, Format and How to Resize

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Photo & Signature Resizer tool →

CUET forms are filled through NTA, and the upload step rejects images that are the wrong size. Here are the requirements and the quickest way to get your files right.

Step by step

  1. Take a recent photo — Light background, face clearly visible, both ears preferably visible.
  2. Sign on white paper — Use a black or blue pen and take a clear photo.
  3. Open the resizer — Choose the NTA preset, or enter the size yourself.
  4. Resize both files — Photo first, then signature.
  5. Check KB before uploading — Photo 10–200 KB, signature 4–30 KB.
💡 If your signature photo comes out too large, compress it with a 25 KB target in Compress Image.

CUET image requirements

  • Photograph: JPG/JPEG, 10 KB to 200 KB
  • Signature: JPG/JPEG, 4 KB to 30 KB

NTA sometimes adds rules (such as name and date on the photo), so always read the latest CUET information bulletin before uploading.

Same rules as NEET and JEE

NTA uses similar image rules across exams. See the NEET & JEE photo guide for pixel sizes and examples.

FAQ

Can I upload a PNG?

No, CUET asks for JPG/JPEG files.

My photo is 300 KB, what do I do?

Compress it to under 200 KB with Compress Image.

Can I use a selfie?

Only if it meets the rules: light background, face straight, no filters.
