GATE Photo & Signature Size (2026) and How to Resize

GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering) has its own photo and signature sizes that differ from SSC and bank exams. Here is what the GATE portal expects.

GATE photo & signature size

  • Photo: around 480 × 640 pixels, JPEG
  • Signature: around 160 × 560 pixels, JPEG
  • Recent colour photo with a plain background
  • Always confirm the exact KB limits in the current GATE brochure

How to resize for GATE

  1. Open the Photo & Signature tool — Choose Custom to enter the exact GATE pixels.
  2. Enter the size — For the photo type width 480 and height 640; for the signature 160 and 560.
  3. Upload and download — Select your image and download it at the GATE size.
💡 GATE updates sizes some years — always cross-check with the official GATE information brochure before uploading.
