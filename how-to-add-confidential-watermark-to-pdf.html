How to Add a 'Confidential' or 'Draft' Watermark to a PDF

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Watermark PDF tool →

A visible watermark tells everyone how a document should be treated — and discourages people from passing on drafts or private copies as final versions.

Step by step

  1. Open Watermark PDF — No sign-up.
  2. Select your PDF — It stays on your device.
  3. Type the text — CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, SAMPLE, COPY or your name.
  4. Choose opacity and size — Light watermarks keep the text readable.
  5. Apply and download — Every page gets the watermark.
💡 Sending an ID copy? Watermark it with the purpose and date, e.g. 'FOR APARTMENT RENTAL ONLY – NOV 2026'. It makes the copy useless for anything else.

Common watermark texts

  • CONFIDENTIAL — internal reports, contracts, HR files
  • DRAFT — documents still under review
  • SAMPLE — templates and previews
  • COPY — copies of original certificates

Watermark vs password

A watermark labels a document but anyone can still open it. To control who can open it, add a password with Protect PDF — you can use both together.

FAQ

Can a watermark be removed?

A watermark drawn on the page is hard to remove cleanly, but it is a label, not security. Use a password for real protection.

Will it cover my text?

Use low opacity so the text underneath stays readable.

Is my document uploaded?

No. The watermark is added on your device.
