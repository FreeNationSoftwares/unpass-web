How to Add a Watermark to a PDF

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-10-17

Open the Watermark PDF tool →

Before you share a document, a watermark — CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, or your name — makes it clear who it belongs to and discourages misuse. Here is how to add one.

How to add a watermark

  1. Open the Watermark PDF tool — No app needed.
  2. Select your PDF — Choose the file from your device.
  3. Type your text — For example CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, or your name.
  4. Pick strength & download — Light, Medium or Strong, then download the watermarked PDF.
💡 Use the Light setting so your text stays easy to read under the watermark.
Ready? Use the Watermark PDF tool — free, and your file never leaves your device.

FAQ

Is it on every page?

Yes, the watermark is stamped diagonally on all pages.

No — it is semi-transparent so your text stays readable.
