How to Compress a PDF to Under 1 MB (or 2 MB) for Free

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Compress PDF tool →

Job portals, university applications and government websites often reject PDFs over 1 MB or 2 MB. Most large PDFs are big because of scanned pages or photos — which compress very well.

Step by step

  1. Open Compress PDF — Free, no sign-up.
  2. Select your PDF — It stays on your device.
  3. Start with the recommended level — Check the new size the tool shows.
  4. Still over the limit? Use a stronger level — Scanned PDFs usually need it.
  5. Download and upload — Check the text is still readable first.
💡 If one page is a huge photo, it is often faster to compress that photo first with Compress Image, then rebuild the PDF.

How small can a PDF get?

A typical text document of 10–20 pages is already under 1 MB. A scanned document usually ends up around 100–300 KB per page after strong compression, so a 5-page scan can comfortably fit under 1 MB.

If you can't get under the limit

  • Remove pages you do not need with Delete PDF Pages.
  • Split the document and upload it in parts with Split PDF.
  • Rescan at a lower resolution (150–200 DPI is enough for documents).

FAQ

Will compressing make my PDF blurry?

At the recommended level text stays sharp. The strongest level trades some sharpness for size — zoom in to check before uploading.

Is my PDF uploaded to compress it?

No. Unpass compresses it inside your browser.

Is there a file size limit?

No fixed limit; very large files depend on your device's memory.
