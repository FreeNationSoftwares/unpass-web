How to Electronically Sign a Document for Free (No Account)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Sign PDF tool →

Signing services like DocuSign or Adobe Sign are great for teams, but for a one-off lease, consent form or offer letter you do not need an account or a subscription. Here is the free way.

Step by step

  1. Save the document as PDF — If it is a Word file, use File → Save as / Export as PDF.
  2. Open Sign PDF — No account or email needed.
  3. Create your signature — Draw it, or upload a photo of your signature on paper.
  4. Place and resize it — Drag it onto the signature line.
  5. Download and send — Email the signed PDF back.
💡 Need to add the date too? Type it into the document before converting to PDF, or sign a version where the date line is already filled in.

Is an electronic signature legally valid?

In many countries — including the US (ESIGN Act), the UK and the EU (eIDAS) — a simple electronic signature like an image of your handwritten signature is legally valid for most everyday agreements. Some documents, such as wills or certain government filings, have special rules, so check if you are unsure.

When you need a paid e-sign service

If several people must sign in order, you need an audit trail, or your company requires certificate-based signatures, a dedicated e-signature platform makes sense. For signing your own copy and sending it back, a free tool is enough.

No. For a simple signature on your own copy, a free tool like Unpass Sign PDF works without an account.

Yes, draw your signature with your finger in the phone browser.
