How to Fill and Sign a PDF Form Online (Free)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Sign PDF tool →

Application forms, school permission slips and HR documents often arrive as PDFs that need details filled in and a signature at the bottom. Here is a simple, free workflow that avoids printing.

Step by step

  1. Check if the form is fillable — Open it in your browser — if you can click into the boxes and type, it is a fillable form.
  2. Fill in the fields — Type your details, then use the browser's Print → Save as PDF (or Download) to keep the filled copy.
  3. Open Sign PDF — Select the filled PDF.
  4. Add your signature — Draw or upload it and drag it onto the signature line.
  5. Download and send — Save the signed copy and email or upload it.
💡 If the form is not fillable (you cannot click into the boxes), print it once, fill it by hand, take clear photos and turn them into a PDF with Image to PDF — then sign.

Need to merge it with other documents?

Many applications ask for the form plus ID proof and certificates in one file. Sign the form first, then combine everything with Merge PDF, and if the result is too big for the upload limit, shrink it with Compress PDF.

Ready? Use the Sign PDF tool — free, and your file never leaves your device.

FAQ

Can I type text with this tool?

The Sign PDF tool adds your signature. For typing, use a fillable form in your browser, then sign the saved copy.

Will the form stay editable?

The fields you filled stay as they are; the signature is added on top of the page.

Is it safe for documents with personal details?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
