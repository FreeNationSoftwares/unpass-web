How to Make a PDF from Photos on iPhone

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Image to PDF tool →

Receipts, handwritten notes, whiteboard photos or screenshots are easier to send and keep as a single PDF. Here is the quickest way on an iPhone.

Step by step

  1. Open Image to PDF in Safari — No app needed.
  2. Tap Select images — Choose Photo Library and pick several photos.
  3. Set the order — Move each image up or down.
  4. Choose A4 or Fit — A4 for documents, Fit for screenshots.
  5. Create & download — The PDF is saved to Files → Downloads.
💡 iPhone photos are often HEIC. If one will not load, open it in Photos, tap Share → Save as a copy, or take screenshots of it — screenshots are PNG.

Make it look like a scan

Photograph the page from straight above on a dark surface in daylight, and crop off the background in the Photos app before converting. See how to scan documents with your phone.

PDF too big?

iPhone photos are large. Run the PDF through Compress PDF to make it email-friendly.

Ready? Use the Image to PDF tool — free, and your file never leaves your device.

FAQ

Can I combine screenshots into one PDF?

Yes, select all the screenshots and choose Fit.

Where is the PDF saved?

In the Files app under Downloads.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The PDF is created on your iPhone.
