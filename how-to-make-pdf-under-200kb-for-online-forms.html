How to Make a PDF Under 200 KB for Online Forms

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Compress PDF tool →

'File size should not exceed 200 KB' is one of the most common upload errors on government, university and job portals. A phone photo alone can be 3–5 MB, so you need to shrink it.

Step by step

  1. Make the PDF — Photograph the document and convert it with Image to PDF (A4).
  2. Open Compress PDF — Select the file.
  3. Choose strong compression — Scanned documents need a stronger level to reach 200 KB.
  4. Compress & check the size — The tool shows the new size.
  5. Still too big? Reduce pages — Remove extra pages or compress the photos first.
💡 For one-page documents, compressing the photo first (Compress Image → 150 KB) and then making the PDF often gives the smallest, clearest result.

Why 200 KB is hard

Each page of a scan is a full-size photo. A 3-page document can be 10 MB before compression. Strong compression reduces resolution so each page is only 50–70 KB while staying readable.

Multi-page documents

If the portal wants several documents in one file, merge them first with Merge PDF, then compress the final file. If the limit is very tight, ask yourself whether every page is needed and remove extras with Delete PDF Pages.

FAQ

Will the document still be readable?

Yes in most cases. Zoom in after compressing to check the important text.

Can I reach 100 KB?

Usually for 1–2 page documents. See our guide to compressing a PDF to 100 KB.

Is my document uploaded?

No. Compression happens on your own device.
