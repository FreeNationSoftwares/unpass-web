How to Prepare Scholarship Documents as PDF

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Compress PDF tool →

Scholarship portals usually want each document as a separate PDF, clearly readable and under a small size limit. Getting the files right before you start the form saves a lot of stress near the deadline.

Step by step

  1. Collect the documents — Marksheets, income certificate, caste/domicile certificate, fee receipt, bank passbook.
  2. Scan each one — Use your phone camera in daylight and convert with Image to PDF.
  3. Unlock downloaded PDFs — Some online certificates are password-protected — unlock them first.
  4. Compress each PDF — Bring every file under the portal's limit.
  5. Name the files clearly — e.g. Income_Certificate.pdf.
💡 Keep one folder on your phone with all scholarship PDFs — you will need the same files again next year for renewal.

Common size limits

Many portals allow 200 KB to 1 MB per document. Check the instructions page of your scholarship; the NSP photo and signature guide covers the image rules.

Readable beats tiny

Officials must read your documents. After compressing, zoom in to check that names, amounts and stamps are clear. If not, use a lighter compression level.

FAQ

Can I upload a photo instead of a PDF?

Only if the portal accepts images. Most ask for PDF.

My certificate PDF has a password

Use Unlock PDF with the password given on the download page, then upload.

Do I need colour scans?

Colour is safer for documents with stamps and seals.
