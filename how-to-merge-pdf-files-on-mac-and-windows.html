How to Merge PDF Files on Mac and Windows (Free)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Merge PDF tool →

Windows has no built-in way to merge PDFs, and the Mac's method is hidden inside Preview. A browser tool works the same on both — and on your phone.

Step by step

  1. Open Merge PDF in any browser — Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
  2. Select all your PDFs — Or drag them onto the page.
  3. Arrange the order — Move files up or down.
  4. Merge — Everything is combined into one file.
  5. Download — Save the merged PDF.
💡 Rename your files with numbers first (01-cover.pdf, 02-report.pdf) to get them in the right order instantly.

On a Mac with Preview

Open the first PDF in Preview, show thumbnails (View → Thumbnails), then drag the other PDFs into the sidebar where you want them and save. It works, but reordering many files is fiddly.

On Windows

Windows does not include a PDF merger. Instead of installing software, use a browser tool. Unpass merges files on your own PC — nothing is uploaded, which matters for work documents.

Ready? Use the Merge PDF tool — free, and your file never leaves your device.

FAQ

Can I merge PDFs without Adobe Acrobat?

Yes. Unpass Merge PDF is free and works in any browser.

Is there a file limit?

No fixed limit; very large files depend on your computer's memory.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Merging happens on your device.
