How to Merge PDF Files on iPhone and Android

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Merge PDF tool →

Need to send a CV plus certificates, or several receipts as one file, but you are away from your computer? Your phone browser can merge PDFs in seconds.

Step by step

  1. Open Merge PDF in your phone browser — Chrome, Safari or any other.
  2. Tap Select PDFs — Pick several files at once from Files or Downloads.
  3. Set the order — Move files up or down.
  4. Merge — All pages are combined into one PDF.
  5. Download and share — Send it by email or a chat app.
💡 On Android, PDFs from WhatsApp are usually in Downloads or in the WhatsApp Documents folder.

FAQ

Is there a limit on files?

No fixed limit; it depends on your phone's memory.

Will the pages change?

No. Every page is kept exactly as it was.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Merging happens on your phone.
