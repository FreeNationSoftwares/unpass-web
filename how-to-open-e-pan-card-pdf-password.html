e-PAN Card PDF Password: How to Open and Remove It

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Unlock PDF tool →

When you download your e-PAN card, the PDF asks for a password. It is not a password you chose — it follows a fixed format based on your date of birth.

Step by step

  1. Find your date of birth as on PAN — The password is usually your DOB in DDMMYYYY format, e.g. 05081999.
  2. Open Unlock PDF — Select your e-PAN PDF.
  3. Type the password — Enter the DOB with no slashes or spaces.
  4. Unlock & download — Save a copy that opens without a password.
  5. Keep it safe — Store the unlocked copy somewhere private.
💡 For companies and firms, the password is usually the date of incorporation in the same DDMMYYYY format.

Why remove the password?

Many online forms and banks ask you to upload your PAN card, and some portals reject password-protected PDFs. An unlocked copy uploads without errors. You can also turn it into an image with PDF to JPG if the form wants a photo.

If the password does not work

Check that you are using the date of birth exactly as printed on your PAN, with two digits for day and month. If it still fails, check the email or SMS that came with the download — the format can differ for some services.
