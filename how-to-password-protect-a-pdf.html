How to Password Protect a PDF for Free (No Acrobat)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Protect PDF tool →

Salary slips, bank statements, ID copies and contracts often travel by email or WhatsApp. Adding a password means only the person you give it to can open the file — and you can do it for free without Adobe Acrobat.

Step by step

  1. Open Protect PDF — Works on phone and computer browsers.
  2. Select your PDF — It is encrypted on your device, never uploaded.
  3. Type a password twice — Use at least 8 characters with letters and numbers.
  4. Protect & download — Save the locked copy.
  5. Share the password separately — For example by phone or SMS, not in the same email.
💡 Keep an unprotected original in a safe place. If you forget the password, nobody can open the protected copy — not even us.

How secure is it?

The file is encrypted with AES-128, the standard supported by Adobe Reader, Chrome, Edge, Safari and phone PDF apps. Anyone opening it is asked for the password first.

Remove the password later

Need an open copy again? Use Unlock PDF with the same password.

FAQ

Do I need Adobe Acrobat to add a password?

No. Unpass Protect PDF adds a password free in your browser.

Can I protect a PDF on my phone?

Yes, it works in Chrome and Safari on Android and iPhone.

Will the PDF look different?

No. Only a password is added; every page stays the same.
