How to Print a Password-Protected or Secured PDF

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Unlock PDF tool →

Some PDFs open fine but the Print button is greyed out, or every print asks for a password. That is because the creator added restrictions. If it is your own document — a bank statement, bill or payslip — you can make an unrestricted copy.

Step by step

  1. Open Unlock PDF — Free and private.
  2. Select the PDF — The one you cannot print.
  3. Type the password if it asks — This is the same password you use to open the file.
  4. Unlock & download — You get a clean copy without restrictions.
  5. Print the new copy — Open it and print normally.
💡 Only remove protection from documents that belong to you or that you have permission to use.

Two kinds of PDF protection

An open password stops anyone from viewing the file without it. A permissions password lets you view the file but blocks printing, copying or editing. Unpass needs you to know the open password, but you do not need the permissions password to make a printable copy of a file you can already open.

Common passwords for statements

Banks and services often use a mix of your name and date of birth, or your customer ID, as the password — check the email that came with the PDF.

FAQ

Why is Print greyed out in my PDF?

The PDF has a permissions restriction that blocks printing.

Can it crack an unknown password?

No. You need the password to open the file; Unpass removes the protection after that.

Is my statement uploaded?

No. Unlocking happens entirely in your browser.
