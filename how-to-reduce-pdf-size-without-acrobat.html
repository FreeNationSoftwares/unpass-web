How to Reduce PDF Size Without Adobe Acrobat

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Compress PDF tool →

Acrobat's compressor is behind a subscription, and many free websites make you upload private files to their servers. Here is a free method that compresses the PDF on your own device.

Step by step

  1. Open Compress PDF — Works in any modern browser.
  2. Select your PDF — It stays on your device.
  3. Pick a compression level — Start with the recommended level.
  4. Compress & compare — The tool shows the size before and after.
  5. Download — Use a stronger level if you need it smaller.
💡 If the PDF is mostly scanned pages or photos, it can usually shrink a lot. A text-only PDF that is already small may not shrink much — that is normal.

Why are some PDFs so big?

Large PDFs usually contain high-resolution photos or scans. Compression lowers image resolution and quality to a level that still looks fine on screen. Read more in why is my PDF file so large.

Common size targets

  • Email: under 10–25 MB depending on the provider
  • Job portals: often 2 MB or less
  • Government / exam forms: often 100–500 KB
