How to Remove the Password from a Bank Statement PDF

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Unlock PDF tool →

Banks and card companies lock e-statements with a password. That is good for security in transit, but annoying when you need to open, print or upload the statement for a loan, visa or tax return.

Step by step

  1. Find the password — Check the email that came with the statement — it explains the format.
  2. Open Unlock PDF — Free and private.
  3. Select the statement — It stays on your device.
  4. Type the password — The same one you use to open it.
  5. Download the unlocked copy — It opens without a password from now on.
💡 Store unlocked statements in a secure place, like an encrypted folder or your password manager's file vault.

Common password formats

Banks usually build the password from details only you know, for example part of your name plus your date of birth, the last digits of your account or card number, or your customer ID. The exact format is always explained in the statement email or on your bank's website.

Why remove it?

Visa offices, lenders, accountants and upload portals often reject password-protected files. An unlocked copy uploads without errors and is easier to print, merge with other documents or compress.

Is it safe?

With Unpass, yes: the statement is unlocked inside your browser and is never uploaded — important for a document full of account details.

FAQ

Can I unlock it without the password?

No. You need the password to open it once; Unpass then saves a copy without it.

Is this legal?

Yes, for your own statements. Only unlock documents you own or have permission to use.

Can I merge several statements?

Yes. Unlock each one, then join them with Merge PDF.
