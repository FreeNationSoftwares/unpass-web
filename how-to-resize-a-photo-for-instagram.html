Instagram Photo Size (2026): Square, Portrait & Story in Pixels

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Photo & Signature Resizer tool →

Instagram crops photos that do not match its shapes, sometimes cutting off heads or text. Resizing to the right pixel size first means your post looks exactly as you planned.

Step by step

  1. Open the resizer — Free, in your browser.
  2. Select your photo — JPG or PNG.
  3. Enter custom width and height — For example 1080 × 1350 for a portrait post.
  4. Resize — The photo is centre-cropped to the exact shape.
  5. Download and post — Upload the new image to Instagram.
💡 Portrait 1080×1350 (4:5) takes up the most space in the feed, so it usually gets more attention than a square post.

Instagram sizes at a glance

  • Square post: 1080 × 1080 px (1:1)
  • Portrait post: 1080 × 1350 px (4:5)
  • Landscape post: 1080 × 566 px (1.91:1)
  • Story / Reel cover: 1080 × 1920 px (9:16)
  • Profile picture: 320 × 320 px (shown as a circle)

Keep the subject in the centre

The resizer crops from the centre, so photos where the subject is in the middle crop best. For a profile picture, keep your face well inside the frame because Instagram shows it as a circle.

FAQ

What happens if my photo is a different shape?

Instagram crops or adds borders. Resizing first lets you control the result.

Does resizing reduce quality?

Going down to 1080 px wide keeps photos sharp on phones.

Can I use the same sizes for Facebook?

Yes, 1080×1080 and 1080×1350 also work well on Facebook.
