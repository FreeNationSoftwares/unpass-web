How to Rotate a PDF (Fix Sideways or Upside-Down Pages)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-10-17

Open the Rotate PDF tool →

Scanned PDFs often come out sideways or upside down. Rotating them makes the document readable and correct before you share or print it.

How to rotate a PDF

  1. Open the Rotate PDF tool — No app needed.
  2. Select your PDF — Choose the file from your device.
  3. Pick a direction — Right 90°, Left 90°, or Flip 180°.
  4. Download — Get the corrected PDF with all pages turned the right way.
💡 If your scan is upside down, use Flip 180°. For a sideways page, use Right or Left 90° depending on which way it is tilted.
Ready? Use the Rotate PDF tool — free, and your file never leaves your device.
