How to Scan Documents to PDF with Your Phone (No Scanner)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Image to PDF tool →

You do not need a scanner to send a document as a PDF. A phone camera plus the right technique gives you a clear, readable copy that forms and offices accept.

Step by step

  1. Place the page on a dark, flat surface — Contrast helps the page edges stand out.
  2. Use daylight, no flash — Flash creates glare; stand so your shadow does not fall on the page.
  3. Hold the phone straight above the page — Keep the page filling most of the frame and tap to focus.
  4. Make the PDF — Open Image to PDF, select your photos, set the order and choose A4.
  5. Check the size — If the file is too big for the upload, compress it.
💡 Crop away the table around the page in your phone's photo editor before converting — the PDF will look much more like a real scan.

How to keep the text readable

Take the photo from directly above, not at an angle; wipe the camera lens; and avoid zooming — move the phone closer instead. If the text is blurry on your screen, it will be blurry in the PDF too, so retake it.

Making it small enough for online forms

Many portals limit uploads to 200 KB–2 MB. Use Compress PDF after creating the file; choose a stronger level if it is still too large.

FAQ

Is a phone scan accepted for official forms?

Usually yes, as long as it is clear, complete and readable.

Can I scan multiple pages into one PDF?

Yes. Take one photo per page and select them all in Image to PDF.

Black-and-white or colour?

Colour is fine for most forms; ID documents are usually expected in colour.
