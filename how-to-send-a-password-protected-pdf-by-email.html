How to Send a Password-Protected PDF by Email (Safely)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Protect PDF tool →

Emailing payslips, invoices, medical reports or ID copies as plain attachments means anyone who gets access to the email can read them. A password on the PDF adds a simple, strong layer of protection.

Step by step

  1. Open Protect PDF — Free, in your browser.
  2. Select the PDF — It is encrypted on your device.
  3. Set a strong password — At least 8 characters with letters and numbers.
  4. Download and attach it — Attach the protected PDF to your email.
  5. Send the password separately — By text, phone call or a messaging app — never in the same email.
💡 Agree on a password in person or by phone for regular senders, so you never have to write it down anywhere.

Why not put the password in the email?

If someone gets into the email account, they get both the file and the password. Sending the password through a different channel (SMS, WhatsApp, a call) means they would need to break into two accounts.

Will the recipient be able to open it?

Yes. The PDF uses standard AES encryption, which opens in Adobe Reader, Chrome, Edge, Safari, Preview on Mac and phone PDF apps. They simply type the password.

FAQ

How strong is the protection?

Unpass uses AES-128 encryption, the standard supported by every major PDF reader.

Can I remove the password later?

Yes, use Unlock PDF with the same password.

What if I forget the password?

It cannot be recovered, so keep the original unprotected file somewhere safe.
