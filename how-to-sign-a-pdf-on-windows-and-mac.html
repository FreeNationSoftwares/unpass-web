How to Sign a PDF on Windows and Mac (Free)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Sign PDF tool →

Mac has a hidden signature feature in Preview; Windows has none built in. A browser tool gives you the same simple workflow on both — and on your phone.

Step by step

  1. Open Sign PDF in your browser — Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
  2. Select the PDF — It stays on your computer.
  3. Draw your signature with the mouse or trackpad — Or upload a photo of it.
  4. Drag it onto the line — Resize with the slider.
  5. Sign & download — Save the signed copy.
💡 On a laptop, a trackpad signature looks smoother than a mouse one. Sign slowly and larger than normal, then shrink it.

On a Mac with Preview

Open the PDF in Preview → Markup toolbar → Sign → create a signature with the trackpad or camera. It works well for Mac-only use.

On Windows

Windows has no built-in PDF signing. Edge can draw on PDFs with its 'Draw' tool, but the result looks like a scribble. Unpass Sign PDF lets you place a clean signature exactly on the line.

Ready? Use the Sign PDF tool — free, and your file never leaves your device.

FAQ

Can I sign without Adobe Acrobat?

Yes, Unpass Sign PDF is free and needs no account.

Can I sign multiple pages?

Yes, tick 'Sign all pages'.

Is the PDF uploaded?

No, signing happens on your computer.
