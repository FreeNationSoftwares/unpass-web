How to Sign a PDF Without Printing (Free, 1 Minute)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Sign PDF tool →

Printing a document just to sign it, then scanning it back, wastes paper and time — and the scan usually looks crooked. You can add your real handwritten signature to a PDF directly on your phone or computer in about a minute.

Step by step

  1. Open the Sign PDF tool — It works in any browser — no app or account needed.
  2. Select your PDF — The document opens right on the page so you can see where to sign.
  3. Create your signature — Draw it with your finger or mouse, or upload a photo of your signature on paper.
  4. Drag it onto the signature line — Resize it with the slider and switch pages if needed.
  5. Sign & download — Tap Sign & Download to save the signed PDF.
💡 Signing with a finger looks more natural if you sign a little bigger than usual — then shrink it with the size slider.

Draw or upload — which looks better?

Drawing is quickest and works well on a phone. Uploading gives the most realistic result: sign on plain white paper with a dark pen, take a well-lit photo and choose Upload photo. Keep Remove white background switched on so only the ink lands on your document — no white box around it.

Is a signed PDF valid?

An image of your handwritten signature is a simple electronic signature. It is widely accepted for everyday paperwork such as rental agreements, offer letters, school and club forms, invoices and consent forms. Some official filings require a certificate-based digital signature instead — if a form specifically asks for a DSC, use that.

Is it private?

Yes. The PDF and your signature are processed inside your browser and never uploaded to any server, which matters when the document contains your address, salary or ID details.

FAQ

Do I need Adobe Acrobat to sign a PDF?

No. A free browser tool like Unpass Sign PDF can place your signature on a PDF without Acrobat or any sign-up.

Can I sign on my phone?

Yes. Open the tool in your phone browser and draw your signature with your finger.

Can I sign several pages?

Yes. Tick 'Sign all pages' to place the signature in the same spot on every page.
