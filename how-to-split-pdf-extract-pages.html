How to Split a PDF and Extract the Pages You Need

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-10-17

Open the Split PDF tool →

Sometimes you only need a few pages from a big PDF — one certificate from a bundle, or the first three pages of a document. Splitting lets you keep exactly what you need.

How to split a PDF

  1. Open the Split PDF tool — It shows how many pages your PDF has.
  2. Select your PDF — Choose the file from your device.
  3. Type the pages — Enter the pages to keep, for example 1-3, 5, 8.
  4. Download — Get a new PDF with only those pages, in the order you typed.
💡 You can reorder pages too — the pages come out in the exact order you type them.

FAQ

Yes — they come out in the order you type.
