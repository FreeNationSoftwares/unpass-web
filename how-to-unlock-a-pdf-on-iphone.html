How to Remove a PDF Password on iPhone (Free)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Unlock PDF tool →

Typing a long password every time you open a bank statement or payslip on your iPhone gets old fast. You can save an unlocked copy in under a minute — no app needed.

Step by step

  1. Save the PDF to Files — Open the attachment, tap Share → Save to Files.
  2. Open Unlock PDF in Safari — Any iPhone or iPad browser works.
  3. Tap Select PDF — Choose the file from the Files app.
  4. Type the password — The one you use to open it.
  5. Download the unlocked copy — Safari saves it to Files → Downloads.
💡 Add unpasstools.com to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and it opens like an app next time.

Where did my file download?

Open the Files app → Browse → Downloads (or iCloud Drive → Downloads). From there you can share, print or attach it.

Is it private?

Yes. The PDF is unlocked on your iPhone inside Safari and is never uploaded — important for statements and IDs. See our privacy guarantee.

No. You need the password to open it once; Unpass then saves a copy without it.
