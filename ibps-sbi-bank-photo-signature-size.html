IBPS & SBI Bank Exam Photo and Signature Size (2026)

Filling an IBPS PO, Clerk, SO, SBI or RBI exam form? The photo and signature must be the exact size the portal asks for, or the upload fails. Here are the 2026 bank-exam requirements and how to fix your files in seconds.

Bank exam photo & signature size

  • Photo: 200 × 230 pixels, 20 KB – 50 KB, JPG
  • Signature: 140 × 60 pixels, 10 KB – 20 KB, JPG
  • Thumb impression (SBI): often 240 × 240 px — check your notification
  • Photo should be recent with a plain, light background

How to resize for a bank exam

  4. Signature — Switch to the Bank Signature preset and download at 140×60, 10–20 KB.
