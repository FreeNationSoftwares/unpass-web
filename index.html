Free PDF tools that respect your privacy

Unpass is a collection of simple tools for the files people deal with every day — bank statements, Aadhaar and PAN PDFs, salary slips, exam forms and photos. Unlike most websites, Unpass does all the work inside your own browser. Your file is never uploaded to any server, so your private documents stay private.

What can you do with Unpass?

Unlock PDF removes the password from a PDF you can already open. Merge PDF joins several PDFs into one. Split PDF pulls out only the pages you want. Compress PDF shrinks a big file so it is easy to email or upload. Image to PDF turns your photos into a single PDF, and PDF to Image saves each page as a JPG. For students and job seekers, the Photo & Signature tool resizes images to the exact size govt forms ask for.
