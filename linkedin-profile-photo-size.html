LinkedIn Profile Photo & Banner Size (2026) in Pixels

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Photo & Signature Resizer tool →

Your LinkedIn photo is the first thing recruiters see. A blurry or badly cropped picture makes a weak first impression — and fixing it takes a minute.

Step by step

  1. Pick a clear, recent photo — Face and shoulders, good light, simple background.
  2. Open the resizer — Select the photo.
  3. Pick 'LinkedIn Profile' — It sets 400×400 px.
  4. Resize and download — Your face stays centred in a square.
  5. Upload to LinkedIn — Adjust the circle crop if needed.
💡 Your face should fill about 60% of the frame. Profile photos are shown as a small circle, so a distant shot looks tiny.

LinkedIn image sizes

  • Profile photo: 400×400 px (square, shown as a circle)
  • Personal banner: 1584×396 px
  • Company logo: 400×400 px
  • Shared post image: 1200×627 px

For the banner, use Custom and enter 1584 × 396.

What makes a good profile photo

Look at the camera, smile naturally, wear what you would wear to work, and use a plain or softly blurred background. Avoid group photos, sunglasses and heavy filters.

FAQ

What is the best LinkedIn photo size?

400×400 pixels. Larger square images also work and are scaled down.

Why is my LinkedIn photo blurry?

The original was too small or heavily compressed. Start from a sharp photo at least 400 px wide.

Can I make the banner with this tool?

Yes, choose Custom and enter 1584 × 396.
