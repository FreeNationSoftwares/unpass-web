About Merge PDF

Merge PDF joins two or more PDF files into a single document. Add your files, drag them into the right order, and download one combined PDF. Works fully in your browser.

Questions

How many PDFs can I merge?

You can merge many files at once; the only limit is your device's memory.

Will the quality drop?

No. Merge keeps every page exactly as it is.

Can I merge locked PDFs?

Unlock them first with the Unlock PDF tool, then merge.
