NEET & JEE Photo and Signature Size (2026) and How to Resize

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-10-17

Open the NEET / JEE Photo Resizer →

NTA (which conducts NEET-UG and JEE Main) asks for a photo and signature in a specific size when you fill the application. Getting this wrong means your form can be rejected or put on hold. Here are the correct 2026 sizes and how to fix your files quickly.

NEET / JEE photo and signature size

  • Photo: 413 × 531 pixels, file size 10 KB – 200 KB, JPG
  • Signature: 140 × 60 pixels, file size 4 KB – 30 KB, JPG
  • Photo type: recent passport photo with a white/light background
  • Some years NTA asks for your name and the date printed on the photo — check your notification.

How to resize your NEET / JEE photo

  1. Open the NEET/JEE resizer — The NEET/JEE Photo preset (413×531) is already selected.
  2. Upload your photo — Choose your recent passport photo.
  3. Download — It is resized to 413×531 and kept within 10–200 KB.
  4. Resize the signature — Switch to the signature preset and download it at 140×60, 4–30 KB.
💡 Keep a clear, recent photo with a plain background — NTA can reject a blurry or dark photo even if the size is correct.
