When you apply for a PAN card online (NSDL/Protean or UTIITSL), the photo and signature must meet set sizes. Here's what's commonly required.

PAN card photo & signature size

  • Photo: around 200 × 230 pixels, colour, plain background
  • Signature: within the box as specified on the form
  • JPG format; check the exact KB on the portal
