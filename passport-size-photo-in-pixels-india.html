Passport Size Photo in Pixels (India) — Correct Dimensions

"Passport size photo" means a 3.5 × 4.5 cm photo, but online forms ask for it in pixels, which confuses a lot of people. Here are the correct pixel sizes for different uses.

Passport photo size in pixels

  • For online forms (most common): 413 × 531 pixels (3.5×4.5 cm at 300 DPI)
  • Smaller exam forms (SSC, bank): 200 × 230 pixels
  • For printing at a studio: 600 × 600 pixels or higher, 300 DPI

The exact pixels depend on the form. When an exam gives a specific size, always use that. For a general passport photo for online use, 413×531 is the standard.

FAQ

What is passport size photo in pixels?

About 413×531 pixels (3.5×4.5 cm at 300 DPI) for online forms.

What DPI should a passport photo be?

300 DPI for printing; for online uploads the pixel size matters more than DPI.

Is the background important?

Yes — a plain white or light background is required for most passport photos.
