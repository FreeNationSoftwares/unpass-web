Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Your files stay on your device

Every tool on Unpass runs entirely inside your web browser. Your PDFs and images are never uploaded to our servers, never stored, and never seen by us. When you close the tab, nothing remains.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to sign up and we do not collect your documents. We use basic, privacy-friendly analytics to count visits, and we show ads to keep the site free.

3. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can control personalised ads in your Google Ad Settings.

4. Cookies

Cookies are used only for analytics and advertising as described above. No document content is ever stored in a cookie.

5. Children

Unpass is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Questions? Email FreeNationSoftwares@gmail.com.
