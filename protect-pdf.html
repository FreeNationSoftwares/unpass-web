About Protect PDF

Protect PDF locks a document with a password using AES-128 encryption, so anyone who opens it — on a computer, phone or in an email preview — must type the password first. It is ideal for salary slips, bank statements, ID copies, contracts and medical reports you need to send by email or WhatsApp.

How to password-protect a PDF

  1. Select your PDF — it stays on your device.
  2. Type a password twice — use something you can remember but others can't guess.
  3. Protect & download — the new PDF asks for the password every time it is opened.
💡 Sending the PDF to someone? Share the password in a different way — for example, the file by email and the password by phone or SMS.

Questions

Is Protect PDF free?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up, no watermark and no file limit.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF is encrypted inside your browser and never leaves your device.

How strong is the protection?

The PDF is encrypted with AES-128, the standard supported by Adobe Reader, Chrome, Edge and phone PDF apps.

What if I forget the password?

It cannot be recovered — not even by us, because we never see your file or password. Keep the original unprotected copy somewhere safe.
