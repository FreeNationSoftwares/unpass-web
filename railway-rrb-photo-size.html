Railway RRB Photo & Signature Size (NTPC, Group D, ALP)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-10-17

RRB exams — NTPC, Group D, ALP (Loco Pilot) and JE — ask for a slightly larger photo than SSC. Here is the correct size and how to prepare it.

Railway RRB photo & signature size

  • Photo: 240 × 320 pixels, 30 KB – 70 KB, JPG
  • Signature: commonly 140 × 60 pixels, 10 KB – 40 KB — confirm in your notification
  • Recent colour photo with a light background
