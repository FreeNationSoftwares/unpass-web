How to Reduce PDF File Size for Email

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-10-18

Open the Compress PDF tool →

Most email services limit attachments — Gmail allows 25 MB, Outlook 20 MB, and many company mailboxes far less. A scanned PDF can easily be bigger than that and bounce back. Here is how to shrink it so it sends.

How to compress a PDF for email

  1. Open the Compress PDF tool — No sign-up or install.
  2. Upload your PDF — Pick the file from your device.
  3. Choose a level — "Recommended" gives the best size-to-quality balance.
  4. Download & attach — Attach the smaller file to your email.
💡 If it's still too big after compressing, split the PDF and send it in two emails, or upload it to a drive and share the link.

Why PDFs get so large

Scanned pages and photo-heavy PDFs store each page as a high-resolution image. Compression re-saves those images smaller, often cutting the file 50–80% while keeping it readable.
