About Photo & Signature

Photo & Signature resizes your photo or signature to the exact pixels and KB that exam and government forms require — passport photo, signature box and more.

Questions

What presets are included?

Passport photo, exam photo, signature (small and large), and a square thumbnail — the common form sizes.

Can I set my own size?

Yes — choose Custom and type the width and height in pixels.
