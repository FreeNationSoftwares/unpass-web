About Photo & Signature

Photo & Signature resizes your photo or signature to the exact pixels and KB that Indian exam and government forms require — SSC, UPSC, NEET/JEE, IBPS bank, Railway RRB, passport and more. Pick your exam preset and it sets the size automatically.

Questions

Which exams are covered?

Presets for SSC, UPSC, NEET/JEE, IBPS & SBI bank exams, Railway RRB, plus passport, signature and thumb impression.

What if my exam isn't listed?

Use Custom to type the exact width and height; the KB stays under your target automatically.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Resizing happens on your own device — your photo never leaves your phone or computer.
