About Photo & Signature

Photo & Signature resizes your photo or signature to the exact pixels and file size that online applications require — US, UK, Canada and Schengen visa and passport photos, ID photos, job portals and social profiles. Pick a preset and it sets the size automatically, or type your own.

Questions

Which sizes are covered?

US, UK, Canada and Schengen visa and passport photos, 2×2 inch ID photos, signatures, LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube sizes.

What if my size isn't listed?

Choose Custom and type the exact width and height in pixels.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Resizing happens on your own device — your photo never leaves your phone or computer.
