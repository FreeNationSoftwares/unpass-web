About Rotate PDF

Rotate PDF turns all the pages of a PDF left, right or upside down and saves a fixed copy. Useful when a scanned document comes out sideways.

Questions

Does it rotate all pages?

Yes, every page is rotated the same way.

Is the quality affected?

No. Rotation does not change the content or quality.
