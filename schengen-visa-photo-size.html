Schengen Visa Photo Size: 35×45 mm Explained (and in Pixels)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Photo & Signature Resizer tool →

All Schengen countries use the same basic photo standard for short-stay visas. If you need a digital copy for an online form or want to print your own, here is the exact size.

Step by step

  1. Take the photo — Light, plain background, face the camera, neutral expression.
  2. Open the resizer — Select your photo.
  3. Pick 'Schengen / EU 35×45mm' — It sets 413×531 px — 35×45 mm at 300 DPI.
  4. Resize and download — Get a correctly shaped JPEG.
  5. Print at 300 DPI if needed — It prints exactly 35×45 mm.
💡 If you print at home, use photo paper and set the printer to 'actual size' (100%), not 'fit to page'.

Schengen photo rules

  • Size: 35 mm wide × 45 mm high
  • Face: chin to top of head about 32–36 mm (70–80% of the photo)
  • Background: plain light grey, blue or white
  • Recent: taken within the last 6 months
  • Expression: neutral, mouth closed, eyes open

Some embassies add their own rules — check the website of the country you are applying to.

Same size, other countries

35×45 mm is also used by many EU passports, the UK (printed photos) and several other countries, so this preset is useful beyond Schengen visas.
