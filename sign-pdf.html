About Sign PDF

Sign PDF lets you add your handwritten signature to any PDF — a rental agreement, an offer letter, a school form or an invoice — without printing and scanning. Draw it with your finger or mouse, or upload a photo of your signature on paper, then drag it exactly where it belongs.

How to sign a PDF

  1. Select your PDF — it opens right here in your browser.
  2. Create your signature — draw it in the box, or upload a photo of it.
  3. Place it — drag the signature onto the line, resize it with the slider and pick the page.
  4. Sign & download — get the signed PDF instantly.

Questions

Is it free to sign a PDF here?

Yes. Signing is completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on files.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF and your signature are processed inside your browser and never leave your device.

Can I use a photo of my signature?

Yes. Sign on white paper, take a photo, and choose Upload. Keep 'Remove white background' on so only the ink is placed on the page.

Can I sign every page at once?

Yes. For PDFs with more than one page, tick 'Sign all pages' to put the signature in the same spot on every page.

Is this a legal digital signature?

It adds a visual electronic signature (an image of your signature), which is accepted for many everyday forms and agreements. It is not a certificate-based digital signature (DSC) used for things like tax filings.
