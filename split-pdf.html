About Split PDF

Split PDF lets you pull out specific pages from a PDF. Type the pages you want — like 1-3, 5, 8 — and download a new PDF with just those pages.

Questions

How do I choose pages?

Type page numbers and ranges separated by commas, for example 1-3, 5, 8.

Can I reorder pages?

Yes — the pages come out in the order you type them.
