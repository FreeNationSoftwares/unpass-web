Questions

What photo size does SSC need?

200×230 pixels, 20–50 KB, in JPG. Your signature must be 140×60 pixels, 10–20 KB.

My signature is rejected — why?

SSC needs a signature in running handwriting (not capital letters), in black or blue ink on white paper.

Is it free and private?

Yes. It is free, and your photo is resized on your device — nothing is uploaded.
