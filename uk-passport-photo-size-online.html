UK Passport Photo Size for Online Applications

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-14

Open the Photo & Signature Resizer tool →

Applying for or renewing a UK passport online? You can take the photo yourself, but it must meet the digital photo rules or the application will be delayed.

Step by step

  1. Take the photo — Plain cream or light grey background, face the camera, neutral expression.
  2. Open the resizer — Select your photo.
  3. Pick 'UK Passport' — It sets 600×750 px automatically.
  4. Resize — The photo is cropped from the centre to the right shape.
  5. Upload to the passport application — Check the preview the website shows you.
💡 Ask someone else to take the photo from about 1.5 metres away — selfies at arm's length distort the face and are often rejected.

UK digital photo rules

  • Size: at least 600 pixels wide and 750 pixels tall
  • Colour: in colour, not black and white
  • Background: plain, light grey or cream, no shadows or objects
  • Face: looking straight at the camera, mouth closed, eyes open and visible
  • Recent: taken within the last month
  • No filters or edits that change your appearance

Always check the latest rules on GOV.UK before you apply.

Glasses, hair and headwear

It is best to remove glasses. Hair should not cover your eyes, and head coverings are only allowed for religious or medical reasons.
