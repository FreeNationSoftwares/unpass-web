UPSC Photo & Signature Size and Rules (2026)

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-10-17

UPSC (Civil Services, NDA, CDS and the One Time Registration) has slightly larger photo requirements than other exams, plus a few strict rules. Here is what you need.

UPSC photo & signature size

  • Photo & Signature: 350 × 350 up to 1000 × 1000 pixels, 20 KB – 300 KB, JPG
  • For some forms the photo must show your name and the date it was taken
  • The photo should not be older than 10 days at the time of application (for certain forms)
  • Plain background, clear face, no cap or sunglasses
