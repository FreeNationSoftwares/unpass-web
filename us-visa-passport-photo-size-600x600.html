US Visa & Passport Photo Size Online: 600×600 Pixels Explained

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-11-12

Open the Photo & Signature Resizer tool →

The US State Department rejects a lot of photos for simple reasons: wrong size, wrong shape or too large a file. The digital photo rules are strict but easy to meet once you know them.

Step by step

  1. Take the photo — Plain white or off-white background, face the camera, neutral expression, no glasses.
  2. Open the resizer — Select your photo.
  3. Enter 600 × 600 — Width and height in pixels (square).
  4. Resize — The photo is cropped to a square from the centre.
  5. Check the file size — It must be 240 KB or less.
💡 Leave some space above your head when you take the picture — the head must take up roughly half of the photo height, so do not crop too tight.

The digital photo requirements

  • Shape: square
  • Size: at least 600 × 600 px, at most 1200 × 1200 px
  • Format: JPEG
  • File size: 240 KB or less
  • Taken: within the last 6 months, in colour

Rules can change, so always confirm on the official travel.state.gov photo page before you submit.

If the file is over 240 KB

Use Compress Image with a target such as 200 KB. At 600×600 px most photos are well under the limit.

FAQ

Is the US visa photo the same as the passport photo?

The digital requirements are the same: square, 600–1200 px, JPEG, up to 240 KB.

Can I wear glasses?

No, glasses are not allowed in US visa and passport photos.

Can I take it with my phone?

Yes, as long as it meets the background, lighting and size rules.
