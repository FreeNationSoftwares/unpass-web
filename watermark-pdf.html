About Watermark PDF

Watermark PDF adds your text — like CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT or your name — diagonally across every page, so your document is marked before you share it.

Questions

Will it cover my text?

The watermark is semi-transparent, so your content stays readable. Choose Light for the subtlest look.

Is it on every page?

Yes, the watermark is stamped diagonally on all pages.
