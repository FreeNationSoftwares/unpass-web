How to Convert WebP to JPG, PNG to JPG and JPG to PNG

By FreeNation Softwares · Updated 2026-10-17

Open the Image Converter →

Downloaded images are often in WebP format, which many websites, forms and apps don't accept. Converting WebP to JPG (or to PNG) fixes that. You can also convert JPG to PNG when you need a lossless or transparent image.

How to convert an image

  1. Open the Image Converter — Pick the format you want: JPG, PNG or WebP.
  2. Upload your image — Choose a WebP, JPG or PNG file.
  3. Choose "Convert to" — For example, select JPG to turn a WebP into a JPG.
  4. Download — Get the converted image instantly.

JPG vs PNG vs WebP — which to use?

  • JPG: smallest size, best for photos and most forms
  • PNG: lossless and supports transparency, best for logos and graphics
  • WebP: very small size, but not accepted everywhere yet
💡 When you convert to JPG, any transparent background becomes white, because JPG does not support transparency.

FAQ

Why won't a form accept my WebP image?

Many forms only allow JPG or PNG. Convert the WebP to JPG first.

Does converting lose quality?

Converting to PNG is lossless. Converting to JPG uses high quality that looks the same for most images.

Is it uploaded anywhere?

No — conversion happens on your device.
